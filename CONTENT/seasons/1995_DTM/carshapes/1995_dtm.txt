CONSTRUCTION

Base:		Blank GP2Edit, base bitmap from FK's Sportscar Project, 2008 WTCC carset
Editors used:	GP2Edit 1.85, Windows Paint, Paint.NET
Known bugs:	The carshape has a few bugs in it, parts of the car flicker or appear black now and then
Build time:	Around a month.

INSTALLATION

Open GP2Edit.
Load the carset you want.
Click "export to game", make sure the car number positions and camera positions are checked, then "export".
Now open Paul Hoad's Car Editor.
Click "load car", then find "1995 Touring Car.dat" and load it.
Click "load helmet", then find "Blank Helmet.hlt" and load it.
Click "export".
Load GP2.
* Important: make sure you export the car shape, car number positions, and camera positions! *
